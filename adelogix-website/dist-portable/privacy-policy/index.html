Who we are

This website is operated by Adelogix Software Technology ("Adelogix", "we", "us"), located at [registered business address]. For any privacy question, contact us via the contact form.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, and limited technical data needed to run the site:

  • Enquiry details: your name, business email, company, the service you are interested in, an optional budget range, your project description, preferred contact method and, if you choose phone or WhatsApp, your phone number.
  • Booking details: if you book a meeting, the booking provider collects the details you enter there under its own privacy policy.
  • Analytics data: only if you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics collects information such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type.
  • Server logs: our hosting provider may record IP addresses and request information for security and reliability.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • To prepare proposals and, if we work together, to deliver the agreed services.
  • To understand how visitors use the website so we can improve it (only with your consent).
  • To protect the website against spam and abuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your enquiry details for unrelated marketing without your permission.

Legal basis

Where data protection laws such as the UK GDPR or EU GDPR apply, we process enquiry details on the basis of our legitimate interest in responding to you and taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. Analytics cookies are used only with your consent.

Cookies and analytics

The website works without cookies. If analytics is enabled, we ask for your consent before setting any Google Analytics cookies. You can change your choice at any time using "Cookie settings" in the footer.

Who we share information with

We use trusted service providers to operate the website and handle enquiries, such as our hosting provider, email delivery service, booking tool and, with consent, Google Analytics. They process data on our behalf and only as needed to provide their service. [List the specific providers once confirmed.]

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and for up to [24 months] afterwards unless we start working together, in which case project records are kept for the duration of the relationship and any legally required period.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to processing and to withdraw consent. To make a request, contact us via the contact form. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

The website is served over HTTPS and form submissions are transmitted securely. We limit access to enquiry data to the people who need it to respond to you.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website or practices change. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last revised.
