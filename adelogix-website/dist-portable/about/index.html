Our story

We want to be the development partner we would hire ourselves: clear about what will be delivered, honest about trade-offs, and still around after launch.

Many businesses have a story about a software project that ran late, went over budget or produced something nobody wanted to use. Usually the cause was not the code. It was unclear goals, missing conversations and decisions made without the people who would live with the result.

Our aim is practical, reliable software that solves the problem it was built for, delivered in a way that keeps you informed and in control from the first workshop to the last release.

Who we are

Four principles behind every project.

The working habits that shape how we plan, build and communicate, whatever the size of the project.

  • Understand before we build

    Every project starts with questions about your business, not a list of features.

  • Say what we will do, then do it

    Scope, timelines and costs are written down. If something changes, we raise it early.

  • Choose the right tool for the job

    Technology is picked for fit and maintainability, so your product never depends on a trend.

  • Leave things documented

    Code, decisions and access are documented, so your product is never a black box.

Our process

How we work with you.

One point of contact, regular progress updates at an agreed rhythm, and decisions recorded in writing. This is how a typical project runs.

  1. Stage 1 of 4

    Discover

    We learn how your business works, who the users are and what the project has to achieve.

    • Goals and requirements summary
    • Users and key journeys
    • Risks and open questions
  2. Stage 2 of 4

    Plan

    We agree the scope, technical approach and timeline, so you know what will be delivered and when.

    • Scope and phased estimate
    • Technical approach
    • Timeline and milestones
  3. Stage 3 of 4

    Build

    We design, develop and test in stages, sharing working software for your feedback along the way.

    • Designs and prototypes
    • Staged, reviewable releases
    • Testing before every release
  4. Stage 4 of 4

    Launch & Support

    We deploy, hand over what your team needs and provide the support we agreed at the start.

    • Deployment and go-live
    • Documentation and access
    • Agreed ongoing support
