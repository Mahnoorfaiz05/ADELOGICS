Client challenges

Does any of this sound familiar?

  • Your site is slow and plugin-heavy

    Dozens of plugins and a heavy theme make every page load slowly and every update risky.

  • Editing breaks the layout

    Your team avoids making changes because one wrong click in the page builder breaks the design.

  • Security worries keep coming up

    Outdated plugins, unclear hosting and no backup plan leave the site exposed.

  • You need features a theme cannot provide

    Bookings, member areas or custom product options do not fit the theme you bought.

Our solution

Custom themes, clean editing, fewer plugins

We build lightweight custom themes based on your design, with editing blocks set up for the content your team actually publishes. Where functionality is needed, we use well-maintained plugins or write focused custom code, and we set up updates, backups and security hardening from the start.

  • Custom block-based themes built from your design
  • Editor-friendly content blocks with guardrails
  • Minimal, well-maintained plugin set
  • Speed, security hardening and backups configured at launch

Our process

How the work runs, stage by stage.

Every project follows the same four stages, so you always know where things stand and what comes next.

  1. Stage 1 of 4

    Discover

    We review your current site, content, plugins and hosting, and agree what the new site needs to do.

  2. Stage 2 of 4

    Plan

    Templates, content blocks, required plugins and the migration approach are defined.

  3. Stage 3 of 4

    Build

    The theme is developed on a staging site where your team can review pages and try the editor.

  4. Stage 4 of 4

    Launch & Support

    We migrate content, set up redirects, launch and keep WordPress, plugins and backups up to date as agreed.

FAQ

WordPress Development: common questions

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly and we will give you a straight answer.

Ask a question
Do you use page builders?

We generally build custom blocks for the WordPress editor instead of heavy page builders. Pages stay fast and editors cannot accidentally break the layout.

Can you speed up our existing WordPress site?

Yes. We audit themes, plugins, images and hosting, then fix the biggest causes of slow loading.

Can we move from another platform to WordPress?

Yes. We migrate pages, posts, media and SEO settings, and set up redirects so you keep your search visibility.

Do you offer WordPress maintenance?

Yes. Maintenance can include core and plugin updates, backups, uptime checks and small content changes, depending on the plan agreed.
