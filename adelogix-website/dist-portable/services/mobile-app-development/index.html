Client challenges

Does any of this sound familiar?

  • Your customers expect an app, not a mobile website

    Repeat customers want faster access, saved details and notifications. A browser bookmark does not give them that.

  • Field teams work from paper or messaging apps

    Information gets re-typed back at the office, photos go missing and nobody has a reliable picture of what happened on site.

  • Two platforms feel like two projects

    Building separately for iOS and Android doubles the cost and the coordination, and features drift apart over time.

  • An earlier app was abandoned or poorly rated

    Crashes, confusing navigation or slow load times have hurt reviews, and users have stopped opening it.

Our solution

Apps planned around the moment of use

We start by mapping when and where people will open the app, then design flows that respect that moment: one hand, a poor signal, thirty seconds to spare. Depending on your needs we recommend a cross-platform approach that shares one codebase across iOS and Android, or native development where the product depends on deep device features.

  • Clickable prototype tested before development begins
  • One shared codebase for iOS and Android where it makes sense
  • Backend, APIs and admin panel built alongside the app
  • App Store and Google Play submission handled for you

Our process

How the work runs, stage by stage.

Every project follows the same four stages, so you always know where things stand and what comes next.

  1. Stage 1 of 4

    Discover

    We define who uses the app, what they need to do in it and which device features, such as camera, location or payments, are involved.

  2. Stage 2 of 4

    Plan

    Screens, user flows and the platform approach are agreed, along with the backend the app depends on.

  3. Stage 3 of 4

    Build

    You receive test builds on your own phone throughout development, so feedback happens early and often.

  4. Stage 4 of 4

    Launch & Support

    We prepare store listings, manage review submissions and support OS updates and new releases as agreed.

FAQ

Mobile App Development: common questions

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly and we will give you a straight answer.

Ask a question
Should we build for iOS, Android or both?

It depends on who your users are. We look at your audience and budget and usually recommend a cross-platform build so both platforms launch together. Native development makes sense when an app relies heavily on device-specific features.

Do you publish the app to the stores?

Yes. We prepare the listing assets, handle submission and respond to review feedback from Apple and Google. The app is published under your own developer accounts.

Can the app work without an internet connection?

It can, if the use case needs it. Offline support is planned from the start, because it affects how data is stored and synchronised.

What happens after launch?

Mobile platforms change every year. We offer agreed support for fixes, OS compatibility updates and new features, so the app keeps working as devices change.
