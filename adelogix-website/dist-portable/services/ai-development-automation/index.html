Client challenges

Does any of this sound familiar?

  • Your team copies information between systems

    Leads, orders and documents move by hand from email to CRM to spreadsheet, and each step adds delay and errors.

  • Documents take too long to process

    Invoices, forms or applications are read and keyed in manually before anyone can act on them.

  • You want to use AI but do not know where to start

    There is pressure to "do something with AI", but no clear use case that is worth the investment.

  • Customers wait for simple answers

    Your team answers the same questions repeatedly, and response times suffer during busy periods.

Our solution

Start with the task, not the technology

We look for the specific, repeated tasks where AI or automation can save time reliably, then build them into the systems your team already uses. AI features are designed with human review where accuracy matters, and every automation is logged so you can see what ran and why.

  • Use case review to find work worth automating
  • AI features built into your existing apps and workflows
  • Human review steps where accuracy and judgement matter
  • Logging and monitoring so automation stays transparent

Our process

How the work runs, stage by stage.

Every project follows the same four stages, so you always know where things stand and what comes next.

  1. Stage 1 of 4

    Discover

    We review your workflows, data and tools to identify tasks where automation would make a measurable difference.

  2. Stage 2 of 4

    Plan

    We define the use case, the data involved, accuracy expectations and how people will review results.

  3. Stage 3 of 4

    Build

    A working version is tested on your real examples, refined, and connected to your systems.

  4. Stage 4 of 4

    Launch & Support

    We monitor results after launch, adjust prompts and rules, and extend automation to further tasks as agreed.

FAQ

AI Development & Automation: common questions

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly and we will give you a straight answer.

Ask a question
Is our data safe if we use AI?

We choose providers and configurations based on your data requirements, avoid sending sensitive data where it is not needed, and document exactly where data flows.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

We design for that. Where mistakes would be costly, results go to a person for review before anything is sent or saved.

Do we need a large amount of data to start?

Not usually. Many useful AI features work with your existing documents and a modest set of real examples for testing.

Can you automate work across tools we already pay for?

In most cases. Where tools have APIs or integration platforms, we can connect them and remove manual handovers.
