Client challenges

Does any of this sound familiar?

  • Users get lost or give up

    Sign-ups stall halfway, support tickets ask the same questions, and analytics show people dropping off on the same screens.

  • The product grew without a plan

    Each new feature added its own buttons, colours and patterns, and the interface now feels inconsistent.

  • Developers are guessing

    Designs arrive without states, edge cases or spacing rules, so the built product drifts from what was approved.

  • Stakeholders cannot picture the idea

    A written brief is not enough to align a team or convince investors. You need something people can click through.

Our solution

Structure first, then style

Good interfaces come from clear thinking about who is using them and what they are trying to finish. We map user flows and wireframes first, test them, and only then move to visual design. Everything is built as reusable Figma components, so the design stays consistent as the product grows.

  • User flows and wireframes agreed before visual design
  • Interactive Figma prototypes for testing and stakeholder review
  • Component-based design systems with documented states
  • Developer handoff with specs, assets and responsive behaviour

Our process

How the work runs, stage by stage.

Every project follows the same four stages, so you always know where things stand and what comes next.

  1. Stage 1 of 4

    Discover

    We review your users, goals, existing product and analytics to find where the experience is breaking down.

  2. Stage 2 of 4

    Plan

    Information architecture, user flows and wireframes define what goes where before any styling.

  3. Stage 3 of 4

    Build

    Visual design, interactive prototypes and a component library are created and reviewed in rounds.

  4. Stage 4 of 4

    Launch & Support

    We hand over organised Figma files, support developers during build and review the result against the design.

FAQ

UI/UX Design: common questions

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly and we will give you a straight answer.

Ask a question
Do you only design, or can you also build what you design?

Both. Many clients have us design and develop, which avoids handoff gaps. We are also comfortable handing designs to your in-house developers with full specifications.

What do we receive at the end?

Organised Figma files with all screens and states, a component library, an interactive prototype and handoff notes for development.

Can you redesign part of an existing product?

Yes. We often start with the flows causing the most friction, such as onboarding or checkout, and design improvements that fit your current system.

How many revision rounds are included?

Revision rounds are agreed in the proposal for each project. Because we validate structure with wireframes first, late-stage revisions are usually small.
