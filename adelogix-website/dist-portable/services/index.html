Our process

Our development process.

Every project follows the same four stages, so you always know where things stand and what comes next.

  1. Stage 1 of 4

    Discover

    We learn how your business works, who the users are and what the project has to achieve.

    • Goals and requirements summary
    • Users and key journeys
    • Risks and open questions
  2. Stage 2 of 4

    Plan

    We agree the scope, technical approach and timeline, so you know what will be delivered and when.

    • Scope and phased estimate
    • Technical approach
    • Timeline and milestones
  3. Stage 3 of 4

    Build

    We design, develop and test in stages, sharing working software for your feedback along the way.

    • Designs and prototypes
    • Staged, reviewable releases
    • Testing before every release
  4. Stage 4 of 4

    Launch & Support

    We deploy, hand over what your team needs and provide the support we agreed at the start.

    • Deployment and go-live
    • Documentation and access
    • Agreed ongoing support

Why Adelogix

Why teams choose to work with us.

Most software projects that struggle do so because of unclear scope, poor communication or the wrong technology. These are the habits we use to avoid all three.

How we work

  • Clear communication

    You always know what is in progress, what is finished and what needs your input.

  • Practical problem-solving

    We look for the simplest solution to the real problem, and say so when a feature can wait.

  • Suitable technology

    Tools are chosen for your budget, team and growth plans, not for what is fashionable.

  • Organised delivery

    Agreed scope, visible milestones and staged releases keep the project predictable.

  • Support after launch

    We can stay on to fix issues, monitor and improve the product, on terms agreed upfront.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly and we will give you a straight answer.

Ask a question
How do we start working with Adelogix?

Send us a short description of your project through the contact form or book a call. We will arrange an initial conversation to understand your goals, then suggest next steps, which is usually a discovery phase or a proposal.

Do you work with startups as well as established companies?

Yes. We work with founders building a first product, growing businesses improving their operations and established companies that need additional development capacity.

How are projects priced?

Most projects are priced per phase after we understand the scope. For ongoing work we can agree a monthly arrangement. We explain what is included in every estimate so there are no surprises.

Can you work with our in-house team?

Yes. We can take responsibility for a complete product or work alongside your designers and developers on specific parts.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. We are happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before you share confidential details about your project.

What support do you offer after launch?

Support is agreed per project and can cover bug fixes, updates, monitoring and continued feature development.

Let’s build something great together.

Tell us what you are working on. We will ask the right questions and suggest a practical way forward, whether that is a full project or a short discovery phase.
